PROJECT NOTES / CASE STUDY

The approach

A useful brand system should remain recognisable across different formats and production conditions. Typography, colour, spacing, materials and hierarchy need to work together.

What I work on

My collateral work includes identity applications, marketing communication, stationery and print-oriented design. The emphasis is on practical, production-ready layouts rather than treating a logo as a standalone deliverable.

Project presentation

Specific client projects and final artwork should be shown with permission and paired with a clear explanation of my role. This page avoids invented client results, testimonials or unsupported claims.
