PROJECT NOTES / CASE STUDY

The challenge

In-store brand integrations need to feel cohesive with the retail environment while making the brand and product story easy to understand. Mockups help teams review scale, placement and visual hierarchy before production.

My contribution

I have supported Croma in-store brand integration as a primary visualiser, developing mockups and helping communicate how creative would sit within the store environment. The work connects design craft with practical spatial considerations.

Scope and outcomes

This page describes the nature of my contribution rather than claiming ownership of the full campaign or reporting unverified performance results. Approved final artwork and environment photographs can be added when available.
