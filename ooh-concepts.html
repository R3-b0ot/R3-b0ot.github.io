PROJECT NOTES / CASE STUDY

The opportunity

Outdoor media is most interesting when the placement becomes part of the idea. Architecture, viewing angle, audience movement and the physical boundaries of a display can all influence the creative.

How I explore

My concept work uses environmental mockups and large-format compositions to test how a visual could read in a real location. The goal is to make the idea understandable at a glance while considering the medium it is designed for.

Concept status

These are explorations and speculative creative concepts unless explicitly labelled otherwise. They should not be interpreted as campaigns commissioned by or executed for the brands shown in any associated artwork.

This case study uses a typographic cover rather than presenting illustrative artwork as a real campaign image. Approved project visuals can be added to strengthen the story.
