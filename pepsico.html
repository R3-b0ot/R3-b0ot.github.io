PROJECT NOTES / CASE STUDY

The context

Mobile activations and sampling stations need to carry a recognisable brand system while adapting to different environments, surfaces and language needs. Consistency matters, but so does practical legibility at the point of interaction.

My contribution

My work has included adapting Sting, Pepsi and Mountain Dew campaign assets across formats and languages, supporting state-wise activation surfaces and sampling station/canter-van materials as part of the wider team workflow.

Scope and outcomes

This is a summary of my design-support role, not a claim that I led the entire campaign. Campaign results, client-owned final artwork and confidential execution details are intentionally omitted.

This case study uses a typographic cover rather than presenting illustrative artwork as a real campaign image. Approved project visuals can be added to strengthen the story.
